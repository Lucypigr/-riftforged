# Expedition rules

Explore a 2.5D region, kill enemies with directional weapons/gems, collect persistent ground items by proximity, reconfigure equipment/linked gems, defeat the one-time Rift Lord.

Preserved: 27 gem IDs; five freely assigned actions; no support-only casts; action intervals/mana instead of multi-second cooldowns; matched socket colors and connected/tag-compatible supports; four currencies; flasks 40 max / 10 use / kill rewards 2,5,12; 36 initial ordinary enemies plus boss, 44 total cap.

Drops equipment/gem/currency: normal 3/5/7%, elite 80/38/30%, boss 80/78/75%. One gem probability roll followed by catalog selection. Boss guarantees ground loot only if all rolls fail. Successful boss equipment receives two meaningful rare affixes. No free starting currency; starter weapon/gems remain the baseline loadout.

Experiment corrections: basic attacks manually aimed; touch basics released once; pierce does not add arrows; multishot works on compatible newer projectiles; area support changes actual melee reach; cold changes enemy movement speed; distant packs remain dormant; melee windup telegraphs are dodgeable; obstacle contact uses bounded tangent steering; OS/focus cancellation clears input; failed zero-socket recolor is free; boss HUD fits portrait width and hides after defeat.

Movement, damage and loot remain rendered world actions; the objective text reports progression only.
