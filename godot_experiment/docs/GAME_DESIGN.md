# Expedition rules

Explore a 2.5D region, kill enemies with directional weapons/gems, collect persistent ground items by proximity, reconfigure equipment/linked gems, defeat the one-time Rift Lord.

Preserved: 27 gem IDs; five freely assigned actions; no support-only casts; action intervals/mana instead of multi-second cooldowns; matched socket colors and connected/tag-compatible supports; four currencies; flasks 40 max / 10 use / kill rewards 2,5,12; 36 initial ordinary enemies plus boss, 44 total cap.

Drops equipment/gem/currency: normal 3/5/7%, elite 80/38/30%, boss 80/78/75%. One gem probability roll followed by catalog selection. Boss guarantees ground loot only if all rolls fail. Successful boss equipment receives two meaningful rare affixes. No free starting currency; starter weapon/gems remain the baseline loadout.

Experiment corrections: basic attacks manually aimed; touch basics released once; pierce does not add arrows; multishot works on compatible newer projectiles; area support changes actual melee reach; cold changes enemy movement speed; distant packs remain dormant; melee windup telegraphs are dodgeable; obstacle contact uses bounded tangent steering; OS/focus cancellation clears input; failed zero-socket recolor is free; boss HUD fits portrait width and hides after defeat.

Movement, damage and loot remain rendered world actions; the objective text reports progression only.

## Expanded gem families
57 gems total: 33 active/aura actions and 24 supports. New families: moving cyclone and orbiting blades; delayed earthquake/arrow rain/flame blast; forward fissures and locked beam; spectral/volley/tornado/spark projectiles; caustic/fire/cold ground zones; travelling lightning pulses; auto-targeting fire totems and pursuing sentinels. Only explicit constructs auto-target.
All skills consume mana through the shared hotbar path. Delays are effect wind-up, not multi-second cooldowns. Cast snapshots retain support effects until expiry; orbit/beam recasts refresh rather than stack. Totems/sentinels have lifetime and caps, are not separate equipment items, and cannot be damaged in this simplified version. Sentinel steering stops or slides around obstructions; no navigation mesh.
Gem page provides 23 non-binding example builds with socket colors and local ownership hints. Players install gems manually, can combine several actions across equipment, and assign 1–5 freely. No guide click grants gems or currency. Uniform selection from the 57-gem pool dilutes each individual gem chance; overall 5/38/78% roll is unchanged. Natural-progression balance remains unverified.
Skill names/descriptions state simplifications: spectral blade does not return, sparks do not bounce, flame blast is delayed rather than held channeling, cyclone is timed rather than continuous channeling. No claim of exact POE simulation.
